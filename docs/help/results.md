# Results

Everything RotorHazard's Results page shows, as RotorHazard computes it: for each stage its
summary (the stage's ranking when it has a rank method, otherwise its race totals, fastest
laps and fastest consecutive laps), each heat's summary across its rounds, every round's
leaderboard, and the event totals. Heats with no stage sit under "Unclassified".

The columns are RotorHazard's: rank, pilot, starts where a board spans races, laps, total,
average, fastest lap (with where it was flown), consecutive laps, and points on a round
when the format scores them. Positions come from RotorHazard; a pilot it has not ranked
shows "—".

Results follow the day: a saved race, a marshalled lap or a reassigned heat updates them at
once, here and on RotorHazard's own page.
